# TTS Runner — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-11_

TTS Runner is an on-device text-to-speech app. It is built so that your text,
your recordings, and the audio it generates stay on your phone.

## What the app does with your data

- **Everything runs on-device.** Text you type or share, reference recordings
  you make, cloned voices, and generated audio are processed locally by models
  that run on the phone. None of this content is sent to us — we operate no
  servers and have no account system.
- **Microphone.** The record-audio permission is used only when you choose to
  record a reference voice. Recordings are saved in the app's private storage
  and used solely to create a speaker on the device. You can delete them in the
  app at any time.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising or
  analytics SDKs and collects no usage or identifier data.

## When the app uses the network

The internet permission is used only for actions you initiate:

- **Downloading models** from their public hosts (Hugging Face and this
  project's GitHub releases). These requests download files to your device;
  they do not upload your content.
- **Reading a web page you share.** If you share a URL to be read aloud, the app
  fetches that page's article text so it can speak it. The URL is requested
  directly from that website; the page content is processed on-device and is not
  sent to us.
- **Optional local hosting.** If you start the built-in speech server, your
  phone serves an API to other devices on your own network. This is off by
  default, runs only while you enable it, and sends nothing to us.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone.
